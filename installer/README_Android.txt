Which phones and tablets?
  Android 9 or later, with a 64-bit (arm64) processor - almost every phone
  and tablet from 2018 on. 6 GB of memory or more is recommended.
  Newer phones (about 2019 on) get a faster engine automatically.

Install
  1. Copy TUNAS_<version>_android.apk to the phone (or download it there).
  2. Open it. Android asks to allow installing apps from this source
     (your Files app or browser): allow it, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, choose "More details",
     then "Install anyway". TUNAS is not on Google Play yet.
  4. Open TUNAS. It opens into setup: it checks the phone, recommends a
     model and downloads it (about 0.5 to 1.3 GB - use Wi-Fi).

Update
  Open the new APK and tap Update. Chats, models and settings are kept.

Uninstall
  Long-press TUNAS > App info > Uninstall. This deletes TUNAS's chats,
  models and settings on the phone too.

On a phone, TUNAS works like on a computer, with these differences
  - The AI runs on the phone's processor. Small models answer fastest;
    keep the phone charging for long chats. Answers stay on the phone.
  - Knowledge: pick documents with "New from documents". TUNAS keeps a copy
    of them inside the app (Android lets apps open only the files you pick).
  - Save as Word, PDF or Excel: choose where to save, for example Downloads.
  - Models are downloaded in the app (no USB import on phones).
  - TERAS and cloud providers work the same way, in Settings > Connections.
