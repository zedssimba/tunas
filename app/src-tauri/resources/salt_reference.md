## About S.A.L.T CI (built-in reference)

S.A.L.T CI (S.A.L.T Central Intelligence) is a Malaysian sovereign AI platform. S.A.L.T stands for Scalable, Advanced AI, Local, Trustworthy:
- Scalable: from a single notebook to a multi-site national deployment, on the same platform.
- Advanced AI: local models first, with any major AI provider as an optional connector. Generative and agentic.
- Local: data stays on the organisation's own infrastructure. Fully on-premises and air-gap capable, in Bahasa Melayu and English.
- Trustworthy: audit trail, role-based access, no lock-in, open-source foundations credited openly.

The idea behind the name: like salt in food, S.A.L.T CI enhances people without replacing them. AI advises, people decide.

Who is behind it:
- S.A.L.T. CI TECHNOLOGY is the owner and developer of S.A.L.T CI.
- Pemangkin Teknologi is the authorised distributor and commercial partner of S.A.L.T CI. Its tagline is "Catalysing Sovereign Intelligence".
- S.A.L.T CI was founded and created by CAPT ZEDs.
- S.A.L.T CI and its components are proprietary technology of S.A.L.T. CI TECHNOLOGY, distributed and commercialised by Pemangkin Teknologi.

Who it is for: government agencies, universities, defence and regulated enterprises that need AI but cannot send their data to a foreign cloud, and the people who work in them.

The main parts:
- TERAS: the nucleus of S.A.L.T CI, an AI core an organisation runs on its own servers. It runs AI models on the organisation's own hardware (and cloud models when allowed), answers from the organisation's documents with sources, remembers each person's preferences, holds meetings with people and AI models (Musyawarah) with minutes, and gives one sign-in for the S.A.L.T CI apps. It works air-gapped (nothing leaves the network) or hybrid.
- TUNAS: a free, personal AI for one person on their own machine. It works offline, runs open AI models locally, speaks English and Bahasa Melayu, reads documents dropped into a chat, and can carry an organisation's or a person's own name and logo. TUNAS is the first step; TERAS brings the same AI to a whole organisation.
- The S.A.L.T CI apps: a growing family of apps on the same core, for example WARKAH (email), PENTAS (presentations and live demos), Kembara (group navigation and intercom), NEXUS (server and terminal management), MOM Studio (meeting minutes) and REKA (building apps with AI).

TUNAS compared with TERAS: TUNAS is one person, one machine, free. TERAS is the whole organisation, with shared models, team knowledge with permissions, sign-in and administration, audit, meetings with AI, and the S.A.L.T CI apps.
